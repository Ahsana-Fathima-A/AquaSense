##### **Aqua-Sense: Ai-Powered Iot System For Public Water Quality Monitoring \& Predictive Alert**





###### Aqua-Sense is an IoT-based water quality monitoring system designed to provide safe, reliable, and transparent monitoring of public water supply networks. The system integrates IoT sensors, LoRa communication, cloud computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), and a mobile application to enable real-time water quality monitoring and intelligent analysis.



###### The sensor nodes use an ESP32 as the main controller to collect multi-parameter water quality data, including pH, turbidity, temperature, ORP, and TDS. The collected data is transmitted through LoRa communication to a base station, which uploads the readings to the Firebase Realtime Database for cloud storage and access.



###### A hybrid CNN-LSTM model is used to analyze water quality data and classify water quality conditions. The mobile application provides live sensor readings, historical data, prediction results, alerts, and recommendations supported by Explainable AI (XAI). A feedback mechanism also enables interaction between users and authorities.



###### Overall, Aqua-Sense aims to move water quality management from periodic monitoring toward a more real-time, data-driven, and proactive approach, supporting early identification of potential water quality issues and informed decision-making.
